Volvo FM & FMX — built for safety

Our Volvo fleet incorporates advanced driver-assistance, stability and braking technology designed to support safer heavy-vehicle operations. Feature availability varies by model and specification.

Collision Warning with Emergency BrakeDetects vehicles ahead, warns the driver and can apply braking where required.
Adaptive Cruise ControlSupports a safe following distance by automatically adjusting speed.
Electronic Stability ControlHelps reduce the risk of rollover and loss of control.
Lane Support SystemsWarns and assists the driver when the truck unintentionally moves from its lane.
Driver Alert SupportMonitors driving behaviour and alerts the driver to signs of fatigue or inattention.
Side Collision Avoidance SupportHelps identify vehicles and vulnerable road users in blind-spot areas.
Electronic Braking SystemCoordinates responsive braking performance across the combination.
Stretch BrakeApplies trailer braking under heavy braking to help reduce jack-knifing risk.
Tyre Pressure MonitoringProvides real-time information on tyre pressure and temperature.
