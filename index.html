Who we are

Transport capability without compromise.

Cartage Australia specialises in the transport of quarry products, raw materials and bulk materials. Since 2004, the business has grown around a simple commitment: provide the safest, most reliable and most productive service possible.

Our scale, modern equipment and experienced operations team allow us to support ongoing supply programs, short-term campaigns and major civil and infrastructure projects.
