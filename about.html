Our Story

Growing from strong foundations.

2004Established
135+Truck and trailer combinations
3States of operation

Cartage Australia was established by Wayne Vella and Ray Cauchi with a clear focus on dependable service, productive equipment and long-term customer relationships. From its origins in quarry-product transport, the company has developed into a multi-state operation supporting concrete plants, civil contractors, infrastructure projects and major industrial customers.

Our business combines experienced people, modern high-capacity vehicles and disciplined operational systems. This enables us to deliver ongoing supply programs, short-term campaigns and complex project work safely and reliably—whether the task is metropolitan, regional or remote.

We continue to invest in fleet technology, safety systems, maintenance capability and the development of our people so that we can respond to customers with the scale, flexibility and consistency their operations require.
