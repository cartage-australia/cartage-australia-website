Sustainability priorities

Sustainability is at the core of how we operate. From the equipment we run to the way we plan, move and maintain, we are focused on reducing emissions, improving efficiency and driving positive change.

Higher payloads,
fewer movements

Our high-capacity vehicles carry more material per trip, reducing the number of movements on the road, traffic congestion and overall emissions.

per tonne

Up to50%less particulate matter
Up to5%better fuel efficiency*
